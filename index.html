About me

I'm a final-year Computer Science and Engineering student who got into software the way most testers do — by breaking things first, then figuring out how to build them properly. That habit of questioning "what happens if..." now shapes how I approach every project, from manual test plans to smart contract logic.

Outside the IDE, I compete in archery at the national level — which, it turns out, teaches the same lesson as QA: you don't get credit for effort, only for hitting the mark.
